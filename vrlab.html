VR Science Lab

This Virtual Reality Science Lab is an immersive educational platform designed to transform how students experience and understand science practicals. The project bridges the gap between abstract academic theory and hands-on execution. To make adoption effortless, the platform is delivered as a complete service that includes both the software and the hardware. This ready-to-use solution allows teachers to run risk-free, engaging virtual practicals without needing to worry about the technical setup or maintenance.
