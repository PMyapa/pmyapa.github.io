Fighter Jet Endless Aerial Warfare Play the most exciting newest endless mobile game with most daring experience of a fighter jet pilot. As you fly you will encounter tanks, sensor-mounted anti-aircraft tanks and helicopters.Try to destroy or avoid them to move on. If you go through the sensors of the anti-aircraft weapons, you will be attacked.Some anti-aircraft tanks have a protective shell. You can vanish them by destroying the shell first.  If you successfully destroy a target you will get stars. The number of stars you earn depends on the speed of the plane and how close to the target. One star contains magic point each for hit points, fuel and missile. When the magic points of the respective component reach 100% it will be refilled. You can also collect magic points while flying. By saving hit points, fuel and missiles you can fly further. Once your jet reaches the speed of sound(super sonic) you can get combo points. Then the score and magic points will increase exponentially.  So prove that you are brave enough to be a fighter jet pilot.
